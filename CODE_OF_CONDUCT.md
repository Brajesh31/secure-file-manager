# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project participants are expected to communicate respectfully and constructively. Critique the implementation, evidence and impact rather than attacking people.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate and professional.
- Welcome good-faith questions and differing technical approaches.
- Give specific, actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy and do not publish private files, personal information or credentials.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its technical scope.
- Acknowledge mistakes and correct inaccurate claims.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted disclosure of private information, and deliberate sharing of secrets are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report serious incidents privately to the repository owner. Do not repeat sensitive information in a public issue or pull request.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and the project.
