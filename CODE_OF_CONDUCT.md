# Code of Conduct

We expect respectful, constructive participation.

- Be considerate and professional.
- Focus critique on the work and its impact.
- Respect privacy and do not publish confidential files or personal information.
- Welcome good-faith questions and differing technical views.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and doxxing are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when necessary. Report serious incidents privately to the repository owner.
